## Platform

The clinical agentic engine ingests patient charts and EHR data, claims, payer policies, and drug labels, and is described as chart-native and EHR-agnostic across Epic and Cerner. It operates across four workflow stages:

- [Platform](https://latenthealth.com/platform): Identify (care-gap detection, eligibility screening, site-of-care routing), Access (prior authorization, clinical review, appeal generation, benefit routing, payer outreach), Capture (340B eligibility auditing, leakage analytics, financial dashboards), Engage (patient outreach, queue management, outcomes analytics).
- [Partners](https://latenthealth.com/partners): Health-system and ecosystem partners.
- [Customers](https://latenthealth.com/customers): Reported deployment across 50+ health systems covering 2M+ patients annually.
